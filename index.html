How to install

  1. Tap the button for your app and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file (Chrome shows it at the bottom of the screen, or open your Downloads folder).
  3. If Android asks, allow Chrome to Install unknown apps, go back, and tap Install.
  4. Open the app and sign in.

Android may say the app is not from Google Play or has not been verified. That is expected for apps shared outside the Play Store. Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Only install apps from this page.
